5. Ethereum — value capture path

Not “Bitcoin #2.” The 1% hypothesis rests on:

  • Stablecoin growth
  • RWA tokenization
  • DeFi & financial app activity
  • Fees + staking captured by the ecosystem
  • Network effects & liquidity
At $22,000, ETH ≈ 0.56% of projected global wealth. More speculative than Bitcoin.

6. Flow is not market cap

The $10.05 tn is not new liquidity entering all at once.

An investor can sell a stock to buy gold; a fund can sell a bond for BTC; a central bank can switch between currencies and gold.

Price impact depends on marginal demand, available liquidity and reallocation speed. A small fraction of global wealth can produce a much larger change in market cap — without making the scenario certain.

10. Key takeaways

  • Global capital keeps growing (AI + productivity)
  • Marginal reallocation on a giant stock of wealth = disproportionate impact
  • Gold: inelastic supply → small allocation shift = large price effect
  • Bitcoin: absolute digital scarcity (fixed supply)
  • Ethereum: infrastructure layer, not “Bitcoin #2”
  • 2030–2032 = reference horizon, not a mechanical deadline
  • Keep the traditional core + gradually add new allocation sleeves
A superposition, not a revolution. What share of global wealth can each economic function progressively capture?
